Common questions

How do I import a recipe?

Copy the recipe page's link in Safari or the site's share button. In Supper Charged, go to Recipes, tap +, choose Import from a Website, and tap Paste. The app downloads the page, reads the recipe and its ingredients, and lets you review everything before saving. Some sites hide their recipes from apps; for those, try the site's printable version, or write the recipe in yourself.

Why do some ingredients need a product picked?

Supper Charged matches each ingredient to a product from its built-in list, store brands first. An ingredient it can't match confidently is left for you: tap it on the Groceries tab to pick a product, or paste a Walmart product link. Your choice is remembered for every recipe that uses that ingredient.

How do I add a product from the Walmart app?

In the Walmart app, open the product, tap Share, then Copy Link. Switch to Supper Charged: the Groceries tab offers to paste it. You can use it for an ingredient, or add it as an item of its own, like diapers.

Does the app see my Walmart account or prices?

No. The app never signs in to Walmart. Tapping to order opens your Walmart cart with every product added once. You see current prices and change quantities there, and you check out with Walmart as usual.

What happens to checked-off items?

Anything you check off is treated as ordered or already on hand. Sending your cart checks off what went into it, so recipes you plan afterwards show up as still to order. Starting a new meal plan clears every check.

How do I share a recipe?

Tap Share on a recipe to send the link to its original web page. The person you send it to gets the recipe as published, without your changes. A recipe you wrote in the app has no Share button.

Do I need Apple Intelligence?

No. On iPhones with Apple Intelligence turned on, ingredients are read by Apple's on-device model. Every other iPhone uses built-in rules. Either way, nothing leaves your phone. Settings > Reading Ingredients shows which one your iPhone uses.

Is my data backed up?

Your recipes and lists are stored on your iPhone and included in your iCloud or computer backups, if you use them. Supper Charged doesn't sync between devices. Deleting the app deletes its data from the device.

Affiliate disclosure

Supper Charged takes part in Walmart's affiliate program. When you buy through the app's Walmart links, it may earn a commission, at no extra cost to you. Products are matched by store brand and your own choices, never by commission.
