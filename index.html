Supper Charged

From recipe to grocery cart in one tap.

Save recipes from any site, plan your meals, and get one combined grocery list that fills your Walmart cart, store brands first. No account, no ads, no tracking.

Coming soon to the App Store for iPhone
📖

Save any recipe

Paste a link from a recipe site. The recipe, its photo and its ingredients are saved on your iPhone. Or write your own.

🍽️

Plan your meals

Add recipes to your meal plan, and plan one twice to double it. Plans aren't tied to a week: shop when it suits you.

🧾

One combined list

Everything the plan needs, merged and sorted by aisle. Check off what you already have, one item or a whole aisle at a time.

🛒

Your cart in one tap

Each grocery is matched to a store-brand product first, the budget pick. One tap adds everything to your Walmart cart, where you see today's prices.

⭐

Every-order items

Keep milk, bread, diapers and other regulars in every order. They're left out when a planned recipe already needs them.

🔒

Private by design

No account and no Supper Charged server. Your recipes and lists stay on your iPhone, and ingredients are read on the device.

Budget first. Supper Charged picks Great Value and other store brands wherever it can, and remembers any product you'd rather have instead.
