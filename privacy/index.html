Privacy Policy

Effective November 6, 2026

  • We collect no personal data. There's no account, no sign-in and no Supper Charged server.
  • Your data stays on your iPhone: your recipes, meal plan, grocery lists and product choices.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking. The app contains no third-party SDKs.
  • The app goes online only when you ask it to: to import a recipe page, look up a product link, or open Walmart.

This policy covers the Supper Charged app for iPhone and this website. Supper Charged is made by [[DEVELOPER_NAME]] ("we", "us"). Contact: [[CONTACT_EMAIL]].

What the app stores, and where

Everything you put into Supper Charged is stored only in the app's private storage on your device:

  • recipes: names, ingredients, steps, notes, ratings, and photos you add or that come with an imported recipe page;
  • your meal plan, grocery list check marks and every-order items;
  • the products you've picked for ingredients;
  • your settings.

We can't see any of it. iOS protects the app's storage, and it's included in your iCloud or computer backups if you use them, under Apple's terms. The app doesn't sync to any other service.

You can delete recipes and items in the app at any time. Deleting the app deletes all of its data from your device.

What we collect

Nothing. The app sends no personal information to us or to anyone acting for us, and we run no servers that could receive it. That's also why our App Store privacy label says "Data Not Collected".

When the app connects to the internet

The app connects only for things you start, and only to the service involved. As with any website visit, that service receives your device's IP address and basic technical details of the request. Its own privacy policy then applies.

Importing a recipe

When you paste a recipe link, the app downloads that page and its photo directly from the recipe's website, and reads the recipe on your device. It identifies itself as Safari on iPhone, because some sites refuse other apps. These downloads save no cookies and no cache, so the site can't recognize your phone from one import to the next.

Walmart product links and photos

When you paste an older short Walmart share link, the app makes one request to Walmart's link service, with no cookies, to find the product it points to. When you look at a product in the product picker, its photo loads from Walmart's image servers. Lists and other screens use icons drawn on your phone and contact no one.

Opening Walmart

When you tap to open your cart, a product or a search, the link opens in the Walmart app or in Safari. The cart link lists the products to add, and nothing about you. From there, Walmart's privacy policy applies.

Affiliate links

Supper Charged takes part in Walmart's affiliate program. With affiliate tracking on, links to Walmart first pass through Walmart's affiliate network, run by Impact (goto.walmart.com), so that a purchase can be credited. The link carries the affiliate's publisher ID and the Walmart page being opened, and nothing about you. Walmart and Impact may use cookies on their own websites to credit a purchase. The app has no access to that. We receive reports of credited purchases, such as the items and amounts, but never who made them.

Sharing a recipe

The Share button sends the link to the recipe's original web page through whichever app you choose in the share sheet. The app shares nothing on its own.

Clipboard

While the Groceries tab is showing, the app asks iOS whether the clipboard holds a web link, without reading it, so it can offer to add a copied product. The app reads the clipboard only when you tap a Paste button.

Photos

When you add a photo to a recipe, you choose it in Apple's photo picker. The app receives only that photo and has no access to the rest of your library.

On-device intelligence

On iPhones that support Apple Intelligence, ingredient lines are read by Apple's on-device model, using Apple's Foundation Models framework. This runs entirely on your iPhone, and the text isn't sent to us or to Apple. On other iPhones, the app uses built-in rules, also on the device.

Information Apple may share with us

The App Store and TestFlight are run by Apple. If you've chosen to share analytics with app developers (iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Analytics & Improvements), Apple may give us crash reports and aggregated usage statistics that don't identify you. If you test a beta through TestFlight, Apple shares the information described in TestFlight's terms, such as your name, email address and any feedback you send. Apple's privacy policy covers these services.

Children

Supper Charged is a general-audience app and isn't directed at children under 13. It collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there's nothing for us to access, correct, export or delete on our side. Your data is on your device, and you control it there. We don't sell or share personal information, as defined by the California Consumer Privacy Act or any other law. Questions or requests are welcome at [[CONTACT_EMAIL]].

This website

This site uses no cookies, analytics, scripts, web fonts or other third-party content. It's hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log visitors' IP addresses for security and operations, as described in the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

Changes to this policy

If the app's handling of data changes, we'll update this page and the effective date above before the change ships. A change that collects personal data would be described here and in the App Store privacy label first.
